# De Minimis Thresholds — Source Register (Customs, Carbon and Export Controls, status October 2026)

A curated register of the **public primary and secondary sources** on the three families of "de minimis" rule that moved in 2025–26: the US and EU customs reliefs for low-value consignments (withdrawn), the EU CBAM mass threshold (introduced), and the content-based de minimis of the US Export Administration Regulations (narrowing for strategic technologies). Compiled in the course of research on de minimis as a variable regulatory threshold; each entry gives the citation, the public location and one line on why it matters. It complements the doctrinal analysis in [De Minimis and FDPR Disputes](../enforcements/analysis-frameworks/de-minimis-fdr-disputes.md), which covers only the EAR dimension.

> **Verification note.** Items marked ✔ were checked against at least one independent public report on 9 November 2026. Others are cited as published; confirm against the primary text before external use. Access dates in the original research fell between September and October 2026.

## Snapshot — where the thresholds stand

| Regime | Rule | Status (Oct 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| US customs (19 U.S.C. § 1321 "Section 321") | USD 800 duty-free entry | Suspended worldwide by executive order since 29 Aug 2025; made indefinite by CBP Dec. 26-12 (24 Jun 2026); **statutory repeal** from 1 July 2027 under OBBBA § 70531; suspension upheld by the CIT in *Axle of Dearborn* (Aug 2026) |
| EU customs (Reg. 1186/2009 arts 23–24) | EUR 150 duty relief | **Deleted** by Reg. 2026/382 with effect 1 July 2026; Union handling fee under the new UCC (Reg. 2026/2108 art. 20) pending delegated act |
| UK customs | GBP 135 low-value import relief | Under reform — HMT/HMRC consultation (Nov 2025); policy paper pending |
| EU CBAM (Reg. 2023/956 art. 2a) | **50-tonne** annual mass threshold per importer | Introduced by Reg. 2025/2083 (Oct 2025); retained after the Commission's Art. 2a(3) assessment (30 Sep 2026) |
| EAR de minimis (15 CFR § 734.4) | 25 % / 10 % / 0 % US-content thresholds | Unchanged in structure; zero-percent categories expanding for selected strategic technologies (§ 734.4(a)) |

## How to use this register

- **Status monitoring.** Items A7, A12, B5 and C3 carry dates that change compliance posture (1 Jul 2027 repeal; 7 Dec 2026 NPRM comment close; handling-fee delegated act; next Art. 2a(3) review). Add them to the regulatory-monitoring pipeline.
- **Calculations.** D1–D2 remain the only content-based de minimis still operating; related-party and non-arm's-length prices should be tested and documented before use in a § 734.4 calculation.
- **UAE practice.** E3 is the authority UAE free-zone exporters are most likely to overlook when applying US de minimis: a sub-25 % US-content item may be outside the EAR yet still controlled under Cabinet Resolution 156/2025.
