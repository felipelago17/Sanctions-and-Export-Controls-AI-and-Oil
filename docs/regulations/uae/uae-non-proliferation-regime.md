# UAE Non-Proliferation & Export Control Regime

The UAE's own export-control law, as distinct from the US reach-through controls covered elsewhere in this section. This page records the instruments that could be checked on the official legislation portal (uaelegislation.gov.ae) on 13 November 2026 and flags what could not.

> **Source status:** Instrument names, numbers and the points marked "portal" were read from the English texts on the official portal. The Arabic text prevails in case of conflict. Dates marked "secondary" come from commercial summaries and should be confirmed before citation.

---

## Instruments

| Instrument | What it does | Status of check |
|---|---|---|
| **Federal Decree-Law No. 43 of 2021** on the Goods Subject to Non-Proliferation | Core statute. Defines "goods" as the materials, systems, equipment, components, **software or technology** listed in the Goods Schedule; the schedule is issued by Cabinet resolution; a "Competent Entity" is designated by Cabinet resolution | Portal. Issued 20 Sep 2021, effective 2 Jan 2022 (secondary) |
| **Cabinet Resolution No. 15 of 2022** regarding the Executive Office for Control and Non-Proliferation (EOCN) | Establishes the EOCN, affiliated to the Ministry, as the competent authority for implementing FDL 43/2021 | Portal |
| **Cabinet Resolution No. 97 of 2024** | Executive Regulation of FDL 43/2021; names the EOCN as Competent Entity | Portal |
| **Cabinet Resolution No. 156 of 2025** regarding the Schedule of Goods Subject to Control and Non-Proliferation | Approves the current control schedule; repeals the schedule under Cabinet Resolution No. 50 of 2020 (Art. 3); authorises the Minister of Foreign Affairs to amend the schedule (Art. 2). The schedule states that it implements internationally agreed dual-use controls | Portal (listed as last updated 4 Nov 2025). Effective 15 Nov 2025 (secondary) |
| Federal Law No. 13 of 2007 on Commodities Subject to Import and Export Controls | Predecessor, repealed by FDL 43/2021 | Secondary (Morgan Lewis, Aug 2025) |

## Points for AI and compute

- **Software and technology are within the statutory definition of goods.** Whether a given model, set of weights, dataset or accelerator is controlled therefore turns on the schedule under Cabinet Resolution 156/2025. This repository does not assert a classification; check the schedule entry at the determination date.
- **Free zones are in scope.** Practitioner commentary (Al Tamimi, April 2025) states that free-zone companies must obtain export-control permissions for dual-use products, citing Article 2 of the Decree-Law.
- **Licensing authority.** Permissions are sought from the EOCN.
- **Two layers, not one.** A UAE permission does not displace US jurisdiction over US-origin items, and the reverse is also true. See [Extraterritorial Enforcement](../extraterritorial-enforcement.md) and [Compliance Clinic 03](../../scenarios/2026-uae-inference-residency-fine-tuning.md).
