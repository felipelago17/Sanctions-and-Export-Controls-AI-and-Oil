# BIS — Mahan Airways Temporary Denial Order Renewed for a 19th Year (91 FR 67542, October 2026)

On 22 October 2026 the Assistant Secretary of Commerce for Export Enforcement renewed, for a further year, the Temporary Denial Order (TDO) against **Mahan Airways** and its related and added-respondent network — a denial first issued in **March 2008** and renewed without interruption since. The order was published in the Federal Register on **27 October 2026** (91 FR 67542, FR Doc. 2026-21840) and runs to October 2027.

The matter is the longest-running TDO on the books and the best single illustration of three things this repository tracks: (i) how BIS uses the "imminent violation" standard of **EAR § 766.24** to maintain a quasi-permanent denial against a designated airline; (ii) how a **UAE-centred procurement and front-company network** has been attached to the order through "related person" determinations under § 766.23; and (iii) the **aircraft-as-controlled-item** doctrine — airframes and US-origin engines classified 9A991 that remain "subject to the EAR" wherever they fly.

> **Source:** Federal Register notice 2026-21840 (unofficial XML rendition; verify against the official edition on govinfo.gov). All factual statements below are BIS's allegations as set out in the order. No respondent opposed renewal and no related person appealed.

---

## Key Facts

- **Legal basis:** EAR § 766.24 (TDO necessary in the public interest to prevent an imminent violation); § 766.24(d) renewal; ECRA, 50 U.S.C. § 4820(a)(5). Request for renewal filed by OEE on **8 September 2026**, more than 20 days before expiry.
- **Order:** dated 22 Oct 2026; effective immediately; one year. Signed by **David A. Peters**, Assistant Secretary for Export Enforcement.
- **Opposition:** none received. Notice was given to Mahan Airways, Al Naser Airlines, Ali Abdullah Alhay and Bahar Safwa General Trading.
- **Prohibitions:** the standard denied-person terms — no applying for or using licences or licence exceptions; no export, re-export or in-country transfer to or on behalf of a denied person of any item subject to the EAR; third parties barred from servicing, financing, forwarding or otherwise facilitating.

## Evidence Cited for the 2026 Renewal

BIS carries forward the full evidentiary record since 2008 and adds the following for the current period:

**Boeing 777-212ER acquisition (first set out in the Oct 2025 renewal).** Five ex-US-registered 777s (MSNs 33369, 32334, 30866, 28527, 28522; US registrations N99001–N99005) were re-registered in Madagascar (5R-RIJ, 5R-RIS, 5R-ISA, 5R-IJA, 5R-HER), stored at Lanzhou, China until early July 2025, moved to Cambodia via Jakarta, and on or about **15 July 2025** flew to Mashhad and Zahedan, Iran with transponders off over Afghanistan.

**New in 2026:**

- Three of the five now carry Iranian registrations — **EP-MTE** (MSN 33369), **EP-MTB** (MSN 28527), **EP-MTA** (MSN 28522) — and are in revenue service: on **25 August 2026** EP-MTE flew Moscow–Tehran, EP-MTB Beijing–Tehran and EP-MTA Muscat–Tehran.
- **30 July 2026:** OFAC designated general sales agents of Mahan in China, India and Russia under E.O. 13224.
- **8 September 2026:** OFAC designated entities in the UAE, Türkiye and Malaysia under *Operation Economic Outcast*; BIS states that some of them helped Mahan obtain **three further Boeing 777s in summer 2026 routed through the UAE and Oman**. See the repository's [Operation Economic Outcast case note](2026-08-operation-economic-outcast-iran-shipping.md).

**Continuing conduct from earlier orders** (retained in the record): Mahan services to Moscow in breach of the § 746.8 Russia controls (2022); Damascus services including the aircraft struck on the ground in Oct 2023; the 747 (MSN 23413) transferred to Venezuela's EMTRASUR/CONVIASA (2021; BIS action Aug 2022); Airbus A310/A340/A321 and BAE RJ fleet acquisitions via Al Naser and others; a May 2024 post-shipment verification showing a Taiwanese company's electronic components (ECCN 3A991.a.2) diverted to Russia via Iran aboard Mahan aircraft; planned Minsk service (2023).

**Classification point.** Earlier orders classify the airframes under **ECCN 9A991.b** and note that Airbus aircraft with US-origin engines (ECCN 9A991.d) are "subject to the EAR" through the engines — the standard basis for asserting jurisdiction over non-US airframes flown to Iran. The 2026 notice does not state an ECCN for the 777s.

## Why It Matters

1. **The TDO as a standing instrument.** Thirty-plus renewals since 2008 show BIS treating § 766.24 as a durable denial tool against a designated carrier, re-justified each cycle by fresh acquisitions. For practitioners, the lesson is that a TDO party's status does not lapse quietly — check the renewal cycle, not just the Denied Persons List snapshot.
2. **Related-person reach under § 766.23.** The Dubai, RAK and Istanbul entities were attached not for their own violations but because BIS found them owned, controlled, affiliated with or acting on behalf of Mahan. The same test is the one UAE trading companies face when a counterparty is later designated.
3. **Aviation diversion routes run through the Gulf.** The 2026 evidence — three further 777s via the UAE and Oman, Muscat–Tehran operations, GSAs in several jurisdictions — places the UAE and Oman squarely in the current procurement chain, alongside the Economic Outcast designations.
4. **Convergence with OFAC.** The order expressly relies on OFAC's July and September 2026 designations as evidence of imminent violation — a routine example of BIS and OFAC actions reinforcing each other's records.

## Relevance for UAE Compliance Functions

- **Screen for the a/k/a and address set, not just the names.** The order lists P.O. boxes, free-zone units and towers; several of the entities trade under variant names (Sky Blue Bird FZC; Alnaser Airlines and Air Freight Ltd.). Address-level matching catches successor shells.
- **Aviation services are in scope.** Ground handling, fuelling, MRO, lessors, financiers, insurers and freight forwarders in the UAE touching any listed party or any EAR-subject aircraft operated by Mahan are within the TDO's third-party prohibitions.
- **Watch the 777 tail numbers.** EP-MTA/MTB/MTE and the two still Madagascar-registered airframes are the concrete items; any UAE airport, FBO or service provider dealing with them is dealing with items the subject of a denial order.
- **Pair with the Economic Outcast list.** The UAE entities designated on 8 Sep 2026 are the live procurement layer; the TDO respondents are the historic one. Both belong in the restricted-party set.
