## Enforcement Landscape — Key Statistics (Public Data)

| Metric | Value | Source / notes |
|---|---|---|
| BIS civil penalty ceiling (2025 adjustment) | $364,992 per violation | Or twice the transaction value, whichever is greater |
| ECRA criminal penalty ceiling | $1,000,000 per violation | 50 USC § 4819(b) |
| ECRA criminal imprisonment maximum | 20 years | Natural persons; 50 USC § 4819(b) |
| Conspiracy (18 USC § 371) imprisonment maximum | 5 years | No willfulness element required |
| BIS statute of limitations (civil) | 5 years | From date of violation |
| Voluntary Self-Disclosure mitigation | Up to 50% penalty reduction | BIS guidelines; no guarantee |
