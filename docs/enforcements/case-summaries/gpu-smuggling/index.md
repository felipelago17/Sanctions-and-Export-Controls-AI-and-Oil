# GPU Smuggling — Case Summaries

*Enforcement actions involving unauthorised export of AI accelerators and advanced GPUs to restricted end-users.*

---

## Background

GPU smuggling cases involve the unauthorised procurement and export of high-performance AI accelerators — principally NVIDIA A100/H100/H800 and equivalent chips controlled under ECCN 3A090.a — to end-users in China, Russia, and other controlled jurisdictions. The controlling thresholds (≥ 4,800 TFLOPS BF16 aggregate; ≥ 300 TB/s I/O) were introduced in the October 2022 advanced computing rule and are publicly documented.

Enforcement patterns:

- **Transshipment via neutral jurisdiction** (Singapore, UAE, Malaysia, Hong Kong) to obscure the final destination
- **Shell company procurement** — newly incorporated entities with no plausible business rationale purchasing AI compute at scale
- **False end-user certificates** or AES export declarations listing a legitimate company as end-user
- **Fragmented orders** to remain below single-shipment screening thresholds

Common charges: 15 CFR § 764.2(a) (substantive), § 764.2(b) (conspiracy), § 764.2(h) (evasion); criminal parallels under 18 USC § 371 and 50 USC § 4819(b).
