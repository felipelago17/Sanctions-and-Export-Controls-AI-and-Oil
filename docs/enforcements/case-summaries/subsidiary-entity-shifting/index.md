# Subsidiary & Entity Shifting — Case Summaries

*Enforcement actions where respondents used shells, subsidiaries, or nominees to transact with Entity List or SDN-listed parties.*

---

## Background

Entity-shifting cases involve the establishment or exploitation of third-country or domestic legal entities to route transactions that would otherwise require a licence because a direct counterparty is on the Entity List, MEU List, or SDN List. The conduct is charged under:

- **15 CFR § 764.2(h):** Evasion — acting with intent to evade EAR provisions
- **15 CFR § 764.2(b):** Conspiracy — agreement among the shifting parties
- **OFAC: 50% Rule** — where the SDN party holds ≥ 50% (direct or aggregate) in the shell, the shell is itself blocked regardless of its separate legal existence

See [OFAC UBO & 50% Rule](../../../regulations/ofac-ubo-50-percent-rule.md) and [BIS Affiliates Rule](../../../regulations/uae/bis-affiliates-rule.md) for the parallel regulatory frameworks that the entity-shifting conduct seeks to circumvent.

### Common Entity-Shifting Structures

| Structure | Description | Enforcement signature |
|---|---|---|
| **Clean-name front** | Newly incorporated entity with no connection to the restricted party on paper; in practice controlled by restricted party principals | New incorporation date; shared address/directors; payments from restricted party |
| **Dormant subsidiary reactivation** | A dormant subsidiary of an Entity List party is reactivated under a changed name to conduct controlled transactions | Corporate registry shows dormancy; reactivation timed with Entity List addition |
| **Layered affiliate chain** | Multiple intermediate entities in different jurisdictions dilute the apparent ownership below the 50% threshold at each link | Aggregate ownership > 50% when traced through chain; OFAC 50% Rule applies |
| **Contractual nominee** | A legitimate third party contractually acts as the named party on export documentation while the restricted party bears economic risk | Contract terms; risk allocation; back-to-back payments |

### BIS Affiliates Rule Interaction

The BIS Affiliates Rule (15 CFR § 744.21) closes the entity-shifting gap for Entity List and MEU List cases: entities owned ≥ 50% individually or in the aggregate by listed parties are themselves subject to licence requirements. The snap-back to full Phase 2 coverage on 10 November 2026 extends this to EAR99 items.

See [BIS Affiliates Rule](../../../regulations/uae/bis-affiliates-rule.md) for the current suspension status and Phase 2 timeline.
