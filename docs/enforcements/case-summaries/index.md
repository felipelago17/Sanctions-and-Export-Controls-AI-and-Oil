## Cross-Cutting Legal Standards

Most enforcement actions in each category involve one or more of the following:

- **Knowledge standard:** 15 CFR § 772.1 "knows" — see [High-Probability Standard](../analysis-frameworks/high-probability-standard.md)
- **Inchoate liability:** Attempt, conspiracy, aiding/abetting — see [Inchoate Provisions](../analysis-frameworks/inchoate-provisions-application.md)
- **Jurisdictional disputes:** De minimis / FDPR — see [De Minimis & FDPR Disputes](../analysis-frameworks/de-minimis-fdr-disputes.md)
