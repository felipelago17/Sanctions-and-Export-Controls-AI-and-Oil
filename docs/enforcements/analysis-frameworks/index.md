# Analysis Frameworks

*Legal standards and doctrines that recur across export control enforcement categories.*

---

These frameworks provide analytical scaffolding for reading BIS charging letters, DOJ-NSD criminal complaints, and OFAC enforcement releases. Each page addresses a doctrine that appears across multiple enforcement contexts — from GPU smuggling to semiconductor equipment cases to entity-shifting structures.

| Framework | Scope | Key provision(s) |
|---|---|---|
| [High-Probability Standard](high-probability-standard.md) | When does a party legally "know" of a violation? Three-tier structure: positive knowledge → high-probability awareness → willful blindness | 15 CFR § 772.1 |
| [Knowledge Inference Pathways](knowledge-inference-pathways.md) | How BIS/DOJ build knowledge from circumstantial evidence: structural position, red flags, "is informed" letters, corporate attribution | 15 CFR Part 732 Supp. 3; § 744.22 |
| [De Minimis & FDPR Disputes](de-minimis-fdr-disputes.md) | When does the EAR reach foreign-produced items? De minimis value thresholds (§ 734.4) and the Foreign Direct Product Rule (§ 734.9) | 15 CFR §§ 734.4, 734.9 |
| [Inchoate Provisions](inchoate-provisions-application.md) | Attempt, conspiracy, solicitation, aiding/abetting, and evasion — liability that precedes or facilitates a completed violation | 15 CFR § 764.2(b)–(h) |
