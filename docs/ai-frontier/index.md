# AI Frontier

*Export controls, sanctions, and governance as applied to frontier AI systems, model weights, compute infrastructure, and AI supply chains.*

---

## Scope

This section tracks the intersection of the EAR and OFAC sanctions with frontier AI development: advanced compute controls, AI model-weight licensing, deemed-export exposure in AI research settings, and the regulatory architecture governing who can access frontier AI capabilities.

---

!!! warning "4E091 rescission — model-weight control gap"
    ECCN 4E091 (controlling frontier AI model weights) was introduced January 2025 and rescinded 13 May 2025 (90 FR 20865). A replacement rule is pending. Existing Part 744 end-use/end-user controls and Entity List controls continue to apply to model access for listed parties. Monitor BIS for the replacement rulemaking.
