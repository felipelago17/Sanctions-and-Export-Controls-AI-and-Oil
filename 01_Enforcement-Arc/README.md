# 01 — Enforcement Arc: 1949–2026

This section traces the evolution of US export control enforcement from physical border controls on Cold War strategic materials to logical-access controls on AI model weights and agentic systems.

## The Seven Eras

1. **Cold War Foundation (1949–1979)** — Physical border controls on strategic materials to Soviet bloc
2. **IEEPA & EAR Foundation (1979–1995)** — Dual-use technology; IEEPA emergency powers
3. **Post-Cold War Proliferation (1996–2001)** — WMD proliferation; EAR codified; ECCN system
4. **Post-9/11 Counterterrorism (2001–2017)** — Terror finance; secondary sanctions; financial sector enforcement
5. **Technology Competition & ECRA (2018–2021)** — Emerging technologies; Huawei Entity List; FDPR
6. **Advanced Computing & AI Controls (Oct 2022–2024)** — 3A090; AI chips; China military modernization
7. **AI Diffusion & Agentic Controls (Jan 2025–present)** — AI Diffusion Rule; cyber-predicate enforcement; agentic systems

## Key Insight

Each era extended the *enforcement perimeter* from physical goods at the border to intangible technology in cyberspace. The current era extends the perimeter to **logical access** — the API call, the model query, the agent session. This is not just a new technology category; it is a new theory of export control jurisdiction.
